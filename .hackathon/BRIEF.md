## What judges will reward (plain words)
1. **Innovation 25%.** An original idea *and* approach. The obvious idea for each statement (budgeting app, fertility chart, Semgrep wrapper) won't score here. Find the angle most teams will miss.
2. **Technical depth 25%.** Judges look at how well it handles **difficult cases** (edge cases, failures), not just the happy path. Show the hard cases on purpose in the demo.
3. **Evidence 20%.** Show it works with **measurements**: baseline vs ours, numbers, failure analysis. A demo alone isn't enough. Pick a problem where we can measure a result inside 24 h.

What this means for picking: choose a statement where (a) the smarter angle is clearly different from what most teams will build, (b) the hard part is real code M1/M3 can build, and (c) a before/after number can be measured by tomorrow morning.
